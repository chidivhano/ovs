Oviaas Lifestyle is an exclusive lifestyle brand made in South Africa. Established in 2014, the brand started in the vibrant streets of Soweto, Johannesburg, Gauteng by Founder & CEO [Thapelo. Molotja]. "In the quest to find myself, I came across this crazy idea of a brand & the rest was history". The brand is a mixture of streetwear, lifestyle, art, music & entertainment. Exclusively available to a certain kind of breed, the real 'go getters', the 'hustlers'. These are the change makers. The brand adapts a winning mindset with its values being growth, achievement & sustainability. The brand is a subsidiary company to Oviaas Holdings (Pty) Ltd in partnership with Oviaas Studios (Pty) Ltd. The brand represents the persuit of success in the midst of opposing forces & events it is self-evident that we are 'Oviaas'. We welcome you to a life changing experience, we hope you enjoy the shopping experience

Oviaas Studios is a subsidiary of Oviaas Holdings (Pty) Ltd in partnership with Oviaas Lifestyle. We are behind the creativity in terms of content & designs. As creative director for Oviaas Lifestyle, we ensure that we offer the freshest quality content and garments exclusively available to you. In collaboration with Oviaas Lifestyle we create utilising high quality resources, expertise, experience & contact or network in order to gain exclusive insights into the industry.

Oviaas Records is brought to you by Oviaas Holdings (Pty) Ltd in partnership with Oviaas Lifestyle. We offer music production, mixing, mastering, marketing, distribution & artist management, sound hire, event planning & management. This department also falls under our art & entertainment.
